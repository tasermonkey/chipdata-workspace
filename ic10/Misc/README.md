## IC Nitrice Crusher
[Source Code](./MBA-IC%20Nitrice%20Crusher.ic10)

This was me trying to manage the Nitrice N20 from liquidifying too much and bursting my pipes.  It will keep it pipes at low enough pressure that before the filter that filters out the N20, from nitrogen, the N20 won't liquidify too much for my pipes to burst.  With added pipe volume you could change the pressure as those pipes would allow for more liquid and as filter out the gases, the input pipe to the filteration device will empty, and the N20 will evaperate back to gas.  So just have to manage the stress of the input pipes while the filteration system does it work.

I added this chip to the filteration unit IC slot.
